# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not disclose suspected vulnerabilities in public issues.

Report the problem privately to the repository owner with:

- the affected component and version;
- reproduction steps or a minimal proof of concept;
- the expected and observed behavior;
- any known mitigation.

Remove credentials, personal information, and unrelated sensitive data from reports. The maintainer will acknowledge the report and coordinate remediation and disclosure when appropriate.
